INTERCHANGE DEVIATIONS

The daily deviations message to CFTM: the trains at each interchange point with the remark, NIL where there is none.

Deviations

IC PointS.NoTrain NoRemarks

Type the train and remark on a point's row; the + adds another train there. A point with no train goes in the message as NIL.
