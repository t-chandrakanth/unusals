SCR TI GOODSSOUTH CENTRAL RAILWAY

How to use

Two parts, two buttons: DPWCS LOCO and SCR UNUSALS. The text size buttons A− and A+ make everything smaller or bigger.

Writing a report

  • Open SCR UNUSALS and the tab you need (Loco failures, Stalling, C&W failures, Block detention).
  • The boxes for a new report are already on the page. Fill them and press Save report. Clear empties them.
  • Have a Word, PDF, Excel file or a photo of a report? Press Attach a file or photo, or paste the report: the boxes are filled for you.

Finding and sharing saved reports

  • Choose From and To dates, or tap a date in the list. The reports of those days appear.
  • Press Edit on a row to change it, then Save.
  • Share the days you chose as an image, an Excel file, text, or on WhatsApp.

DPWCS LOCO

  • Locos: type in the Train no or Location box to update a loco.
  • Report: the position report, ready to share. History: choose From and To dates.

Everything is saved on the phone or PC and in the shared database; it works without internet and uploads when the network returns.
